# Requirements Document

## Introduction

An iPad-optimized single-page React web application that assists nurses in documenting patient care through a simulated AI-assisted conversation interface. The app provides configurable checklists, captures structured notes from simulated voice input, generates follow-up questions, and produces readable shift summaries for handoff to the next nurse on duty. All AI responses are mocked (no real API calls), and data is stored in-memory only.

## Glossary

- **App**: The iPad-optimized single-page React web application for nursing documentation
- **Nurse**: The primary user who documents patient care during a shift
- **Patient**: A care recipient whose information and checklist items are stored in mock data
- **Checklist**: A configurable list of observation/assessment items specific to each patient's conditions
- **Documentation_Session**: The period during which a nurse documents care for a selected patient
- **Simulated_Voice_Input**: Text entered by the nurse in a conversational format, as if transcribed from speech
- **AI_Assistant**: The mocked AI component that processes nurse input, asks follow-up questions, and generates summaries
- **Shift_Summary**: A structured, readable document generated from the documentation session for handoff to the next nurse
- **Follow_Up_Question**: A contextually relevant question generated by the AI_Assistant to capture potentially missed information
- **Touch_Target**: An interactive UI element sized at minimum 44x44 pixels for iPad touch interaction
- **Corporate_Trust_Design_System**: The visual design system using indigo/violet palette, Plus Jakarta Sans font, elevated cards, and gradient accents

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Patient Selection

**User Story:** As a nurse, I want to select a patient from a list, so that I can begin documenting care for that specific patient.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the App loads, THE App SHALL display a list of patients with their names and basic identifying information
2. WHEN a nurse taps a patient from the list, THE App SHALL start a new Documentation_Session for that patient
3. WHEN a Documentation_Session begins, THE App SHALL load the patient-specific Checklist and display the documentation interface
4. THE App SHALL store patient data in-memory using mock data structures

### Requirement 2: Configurable Checklist

**User Story:** As a nurse, I want a patient-specific checklist of items to observe and ask about, so that I can ensure comprehensive documentation without missing critical care items.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Documentation_Session begins, THE App SHALL display the Checklist configured for the selected Patient
2. THE App SHALL support different Checklist items for different Patients based on their conditions
3. THE App SHALL render the entire documentation flow on a single scrollable page so the nurse can quickly navigate to any Checklist item
4. WHEN a nurse taps a Checklist item, THE App SHALL toggle that item between checked and unchecked states
5. THE App SHALL allow Checklist items to be checked off in any order
6. THE App SHALL display Checklist completion progress to the nurse

### Requirement 3: Simulated Voice Input and Conversation

**User Story:** As a nurse, I want to enter notes in a conversational format simulating transcribed speech, so that the AI can capture structured documentation from natural language input.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE App SHALL provide a text input area styled as a voice transcription interface where the nurse types conversational notes
2. WHEN the nurse submits Simulated_Voice_Input, THE AI_Assistant SHALL process the input and extract structured notes relevant to the patient's Checklist items
3. WHEN the AI_Assistant processes input, THE App SHALL display the captured structured notes back to the nurse for confirmation
4. WHEN the AI_Assistant processes input, THE App SHALL automatically check off any Checklist items that are addressed by the nurse's input
5. THE App SHALL simulate realistic processing delays when the AI_Assistant processes input

### Requirement 4: AI Follow-Up Questions

**User Story:** As a nurse, I want the AI to ask contextually relevant follow-up questions after I provide input, so that I can capture any information I may have missed.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the AI_Assistant processes nurse input, THE AI_Assistant SHALL generate Follow_Up_Questions relevant to the patient's condition and the current Documentation_Session context
2. WHEN Follow_Up_Questions are generated, THE App SHALL display them in a conversational format that the nurse can respond to
3. THE AI_Assistant SHALL generate Follow_Up_Questions that address unchecked Checklist items when contextually appropriate
4. WHEN the nurse responds to a Follow_Up_Question, THE AI_Assistant SHALL process the response and update the structured notes accordingly
5. THE App SHALL simulate realistic delays when generating Follow_Up_Questions

### Requirement 5: Summary Generation

**User Story:** As a nurse, I want the AI to generate a clean shift summary from my documentation session, so that the incoming nurse can quickly understand the patient's status.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a nurse requests a summary, THE AI_Assistant SHALL generate a Shift_Summary from all captured notes in the Documentation_Session
2. THE Shift_Summary SHALL be organized into clearly labeled sections for easy scanning
3. THE Shift_Summary SHALL include information from all checked Checklist items and documented observations
4. THE App SHALL simulate a realistic delay when generating the Shift_Summary
5. THE Shift_Summary SHALL use concise, professional clinical language appropriate for nurse-to-nurse handoff

### Requirement 6: Editable Summary

**User Story:** As a nurse, I want to edit sections of the generated summary before finalizing, so that I can correct or add details as needed.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Shift_Summary is generated, THE App SHALL display each section as independently editable
2. WHEN a nurse taps on a Shift_Summary section, THE App SHALL enable inline editing for that section
3. WHEN a nurse modifies a Shift_Summary section, THE App SHALL preserve the changes and update the display immediately
4. THE App SHALL provide a way for the nurse to finalize the edited Shift_Summary

### Requirement 7: iPad-Optimized Interface

**User Story:** As a nurse using an iPad, I want the interface to be touch-optimized and easy to use on a tablet, so that I can efficiently document care during my rounds.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE App SHALL render all Touch_Targets at a minimum size of 44x44 pixels
2. THE App SHALL present the entire documentation workflow on a single scrollable page
3. THE App SHALL use the Corporate_Trust_Design_System with indigo/violet palette, Plus Jakarta Sans font, elevated cards with colored shadows, and gradient accents
4. THE App SHALL use a maximum container width of max-w-7xl with generous whitespace for comfortable reading on iPad
5. THE App SHALL provide smooth scrolling between sections on the single-page layout

### Requirement 8: Accessibility

**User Story:** As a nurse with varying accessibility needs, I want the app to meet WCAG AA compliance standards, so that the interface is usable for all staff members.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE App SHALL meet WCAG AA color contrast requirements for all text and interactive elements
2. THE App SHALL provide appropriate ARIA labels for all interactive elements
3. THE App SHALL support keyboard navigation for all interactive controls
4. THE App SHALL provide visible focus indicators for all interactive elements
5. IF a form validation error occurs, THEN THE App SHALL communicate the error through both visual and accessible means

### Requirement 9: Mocked AI Behavior

**User Story:** As a developer, I want all AI interactions to be realistically mocked, so that the app can be demonstrated without real API dependencies.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE AI_Assistant SHALL generate responses using predefined logic and templates without making external API calls
2. WHEN the AI_Assistant generates any response, THE App SHALL introduce a simulated delay between 500ms and 2000ms to mimic realistic AI processing time
3. THE AI_Assistant SHALL generate contextually appropriate Follow_Up_Questions based on the patient's condition data and unchecked Checklist items
4. THE AI_Assistant SHALL generate structured Shift_Summaries that incorporate all documented observations from the session
